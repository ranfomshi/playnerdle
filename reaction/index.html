About the game

How to play Reaction

Measure how quickly you respond to a visual change through short, repeatable browser reaction tests.

Format
Repeatable reaction trials
Typical round
Under 2 minutes
Cost
Free, with no download required

The rules

Start a trial, wait without anticipating, and respond the moment the visual signal changes. Repeated attempts produce a more useful picture than a single lucky click.

What makes it different

The restrained presentation keeps attention on the signal and makes it easy to compare attempts without downloads or setup.
