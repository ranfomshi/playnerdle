Editorial standards

How Bludle publishes

Bludle’s guides exist to explain games clearly and help people choose what to play. They are written from direct knowledge of games designed, built and tested on this site.

First-hand information

Rules, formats and accessibility notes are checked against the playable game. When a game changes, its guide should change with it. We distinguish daily modes, unlimited modes and progressive runs rather than treating them as interchangeable.

Recommendations

Games are grouped by their actual mechanic, pace and input style. Placement in a Bludle guide is not paid for. Any future commercial relationship that could affect a recommendation will be disclosed beside it.

Corrections and updates

Substantial articles show an updated date. Errors can be reported through the contact form. Corrections are made in the page itself so the current explanation remains the authoritative version.

Claims and sources

Bludle avoids presenting casual games as medical treatment. Broader claims about cognition, education or wellbeing should be qualified and linked to an identifiable source.
