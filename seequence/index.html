WATCH. HOLD. RECOGNISE.

A workout for visual memory.

Each level adds another colour until the sequence reaches eight. After the final flash, choose the exact order from four options. Some choices use the same colours in the wrong order; others change only one colour. Three mistakes end the run.

HOW TO PLAY

Hold the colours in order.

  1. 01
    Watch the orb

    Each colour appears for 1.2 seconds. The dots show how many remain.

  2. 02
    Remember the order

    Colour and position both matter.

  3. 03
    Choose the exact sequence

    Pick one of four patterns. Expect wrong-order and one-colour-off decoys.

  4. 04
    Build your streak

    Correct answers advance the level and multiply your score. You have three lives.
