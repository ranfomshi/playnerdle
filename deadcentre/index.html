About the game

How to play Dead Centre

Memorise a target, wait for it to vanish, and click its exact centre across eight daily spatial-memory rounds.

Format
Eight daily spatial rounds
Typical round
About 3–5 minutes
Cost
Free, with no download required

The rules

Study the target while the canvas is passive. When it disappears and the guessing phase begins, place one marker where you remember its centre and receive a distance score.

What makes it different

Changing target sizes and positions make each round a clean test of spatial memory rather than cursor tracking.
