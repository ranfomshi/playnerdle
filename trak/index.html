EYES ON. MIND ON.

Can you follow what you can’t see?

Trak tests visual attention and mental timing. The signal never changes course when it disappears—you simply have to keep its rhythm alive in your head. Each level moves faster, and every five levels asks you to observe for longer before committing.

About the game

How to play Trak

Follow a moving signal after it disappears and stop it inside changing targets on lines, curves, circles and shaped tracks.

Format
Progressive visual tracking levels
Typical round
About 2–6 minutes
Cost
Free, with no download required

The rules

Watch the signal establish its speed and path. Continue tracking it mentally after it vanishes, then stop it when you believe it has entered the target zone.

What makes it different

Later tracks add curves, loops, letter-like paths and smaller stopping zones, expanding one simple timing action into a varied focus challenge.
