EYES ON. MIND ON.

Can you follow what you can’t see?

Trak tests visual attention and mental timing. The signal never changes course when it disappears—you simply have to keep its rhythm alive in your head. Each level moves faster, and every five levels asks you to observe for longer before committing.

HOW TO PLAY

Follow it—even when it’s gone.

  1. 01
    Watch

    Lock onto the signal as it bounces along the lane.

  2. 02
    Track

    It will fade away. Keep following its speed and direction in your mind.

  3. 03
    Stop

    Tap the lane or press Space when you think its centre is inside the target.

You have three lives. Every hit raises the speed.
