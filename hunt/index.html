SCAN. DEDUCE. DISCOVER.

Every clue narrows the map.

XY is a spatial deduction game built around coordinates without the algebra homework. Each scan gives you a compass direction and grid distance; combine the clues in your head and commit to the coordinate where they intersect.

 HOW TO PLAY

Three ideas. No formulas.

  1. 01
    Scan a square

    Choose any coordinate on the map.

  2. 02
    Read the clue

    “North-east · 4 steps” means the treasure is above, to the right, and four grid moves away.

  3. 03
    Combine your clues

    Each scan rules out more coordinates. Find where every direction and distance intersects.
