SCAN. DEDUCE. DISCOVER.

Every clue narrows the map.

XY is a spatial deduction game built around coordinates without the algebra homework. Each scan gives you a compass direction and grid distance; combine the clues in your head and commit to the coordinate where they intersect.

About the game

How to play XY Marks the Spot

Use coordinate, direction and distance clues to locate hidden treasure before your limited scans run out.

Format
Guided demo then puzzle rounds
Typical round
About 3–6 minutes
Cost
Free, with no download required

The rules

Choose a coordinate to scan. The game reports the direction and distance to the hidden point; combine successive clues to eliminate locations and make the final find.

What makes it different

The required demonstration teaches the coordinate idea with visible possibilities, while real rounds remove those highlights and leave the deduction to you.
