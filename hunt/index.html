How to Play

Find the hidden treasure on the grid before you run out of turns.

  • Click any cell to move your player (blue square) there.
  • You have 3 turns — choose your moves carefully!
  • After each move you get two distance hints. Use them together to work out exactly where the treasure is.

Understanding the hints:

  • Across + Down — the total number of columns and rows between you and the treasure added together.
  • Across − Down — the difference between those two distances (always positive).

Worked example:

You are at column 2, row 2. The hint shows:
Across + Down = 7    Across − Down = 3

Add them: 7 + 3 = 10, divide by 2 → 5 columns across
Subtract them: 7 − 3 = 4, divide by 2 → 2 rows down

So the treasure is 5 columns and 2 rows from you — or 2 columns and 5 rows.
Try both spots. One will be the treasure!

Good luck and happy hunting! 🗺️
