How to play Arc Shift

The rules

Start the runner, then tap, click or press Space only while it is inside the bright target arc. Every hit scores, reverses direction and makes the next target smaller; three misses end the run.

What makes it different

A single action creates a demanding rhythm game because every success changes the direction, target position and timing window. There are no controls to learn, but the precision ceiling keeps rising.
