About the game

How to play Borrowed Letters

Repair five clue answers whose misplaced letters have been passed around a circular chain.

Format
Daily circular word puzzle
Typical round
About 5–8 minutes
Cost
Free, with no download required

The rules

Solve each clue, spot the letter that does not belong, and move the borrowed letters between neighbouring answers until every word is repaired.

What makes it different

Every answer affects two others, so progress comes from combining vocabulary with whole-system reasoning rather than solving clues in isolation.
