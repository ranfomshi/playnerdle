TRAIN YOUR COLOUR SENSE

Less calculation. More intuition.

Every round gives you a target colour and three controls: hue, saturation and brightness. Get within 30 total points to advance. Fast, accurate matches build bigger combos and higher scores—but the clock gets shorter every level.

HOW TO PLAY

Match colour by instinct.

  1. 01
    Study the target

    Use the left swatch as your reference.

  2. 02
    Mix your colour

    Hue chooses the colour family, saturation controls intensity, and brightness moves from dark to light.

  3. 03
    Lock it in

    A total error of 30 or less clears the round. You have three lives.

  4. 04
    Build a combo

    Accurate, fast matches earn more points. Each new level gives you less time.
