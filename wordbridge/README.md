# Word Bridge

Word Bridge is a daily linking-word puzzle. The date in Europe/London selects one of the curated puzzles and progress is stored locally for that date.

The game includes:

- one bridge word connecting two outer words;
- three attempts with a clue after the first miss;
- bridge planks that fill as the answer is typed;
- a completed crossing and phrase reveal on success;
- responsive and reduced-motion behaviour.
