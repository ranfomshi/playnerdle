The rules

Type one word that connects both sides. For RAIN and TIE, BOW creates RAINBOW and BOW TIE. You have three attempts, and a clue appears after the first miss.

What makes it different

Every answer must work in two directions at once. The animated crossing turns that language link into a bridge you visibly complete.
